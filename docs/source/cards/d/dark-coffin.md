# Dark Coffin

## Edisonformat.net (Revised, Post-UTW Rulings)

Source: [https://edisonformat.net/card?name=Dark%20Coffin](https://edisonformat.net/card?name=Dark%20Coffin)

### Edison-Accurate PSCT

> If this face-down card is destroyed and sent to the GY, your opponent chooses one of the following effects:
> ● They discard 1 random card from their hand.
> ● They destroy one monster they control.

### Card Rulings

*   The opponent cannot choose to activate an effect they cannot fulfill. If they have 1 card in hand but control no monsters they must discard 1 card.

*   If this face-down card is attacked and destroyed (e.g. if Special Summoned by "Magical Hats"), this effect will not activate because it was face-up when it was sent to the GY.

*   This effect cannot be negated by "Jinzo" or "Solemn Judgment".
